Current Market Regime

Dominant Narrative

Treasury liquidity support — a decision to at least double long-duration debt buybacks — plus three consecutive soft data prints (jobs, CPI, PPI) have pulled the market's implied hike odds from 50% to 31% this month. That single lever is repricing duration, gold, and Bitcoin together: Gold is up ~10% in August (best month since January) and Bitcoin is up ~22% on the week, both credited to the same yields-down / dollar-down mechanism. Jackson Hole (Aug 27–29) is the fulcrum — new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's first keynote as chair (Fri Aug 28 AM) is the first real test of whether that dovish read holds.

What Could Invalidate It

A hawkish-leaning Warsh keynote, any sign the Treasury slows its buyback pace, or a hot September CPI print would unwind the yields-down thesis quickly — symmetrically bad for TLT, Gold, and Bitcoin, since all three are currently trading the same macro driver rather than independent stories.
